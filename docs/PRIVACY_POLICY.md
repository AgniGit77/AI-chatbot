# AI Fashion Chatbot - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 31, 2024**

## Introduction

At AI Fashion Chatbot ("we", "our", "us"), we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our fashion assistance application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:
- Create an account (optional)
- Enter body measurements
- Upload photos
- Save outfit preferences
- Contact support

**Types of personal information:**
- Name and email (optional)
- Body measurements (height, weight, chest, waist, hip, shoulder width)
- Photos you upload
- Style preferences
- Chat conversation history

### Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain information when you use our service:
- Browser type and version
- Device information
- IP address (anonymized)
- Usage patterns and interactions
- Session data

### Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for:
- Session management
- Authentication
- User preferences
- Security

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may limit functionality.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

### Provide Services
- Generate virtual try-on visualizations
- Provide size recommendations
- Offer styling advice
- Save your preferences and history
- Improve user experience

### Improve Our Service
- Analyze usage patterns
- Debug and fix issues
- Develop new features
- Train and improve AI models

### Security
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Protect user accounts
- Ensure platform security

## Data Storage and Security

### Security Measures

We implement robust security measures to protect your data:

**Encryption:**
- All sensitive data encrypted at rest using AES-256
- Photos and measurements encrypted before storage
- HTTPS/TLS for data in transit
- Secure database connections

**Access Controls:**
- Role-based access control
- Multi-factor authentication for admin access
- Regular security audits
- Penetration testing

**Infrastructure:**
- Secure cloud hosting
- Regular backups
- Disaster recovery procedures
- Firewall protection

### Data Retention

- **Anonymous Users**: Data deleted when session ends
- **Registered Users**: Data retained for 365 days of inactivity
- **Deleted Data**: Permanently removed within 30 days
- **Backups**: Retained for 90 days

## Your Rights and Choices

### Data Access and Portability

You have the right to:
- **Access** your data at any time
- **Export** your data in JSON format
- **Receive** a copy of all information we hold about you

**How to exercise:** Click the privacy icon and select "Export My Data"

### Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:
- **Specific Data Types**: Delete photos, measurements, or history separately
- **Complete Deletion**: Delete all data and close your account
- **Permanent**: Deletion is irreversible

**How to exercise:** Privacy settings → "Delete My Data"

### Data Correction

You can update or correct your information:
- Measurements can be updated in Settings
- Preferences can be modified anytime
- Contact support for assistance

### Opt-Out Rights

You can opt out of:
- Data collection (use anonymous mode)
- Analytics tracking
- Non-essential features

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do NOT Share Your Data

**We never share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent.**

### Limited Exceptions

We may disclose information only when:
- **Required by Law**: Legal obligations, court orders
- **Safety**: To protect rights, property, or safety
- **Business Transfer**: In case of merger or acquisition (with user notification)
- **Your Consent**: When you explicitly authorize sharing

### Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Cloud Hosting**: For infrastructure (data encrypted)
- **Analytics**: For anonymous usage statistics
- **AI APIs**: For enhanced features (optional, data anonymized)

These services are bound by strict data processing agreements.

## Anonymous Mode

### What is Anonymous Mode?

You can use our service without providing any personal information:
- No account required
- No email needed
- Temporary session only
- Data deleted after session

### Limitations

In anonymous mode:
- Cannot save preferences long-term
- Cannot access history after session ends
- Limited personalization
